About Science

Science helps us understand the natural world through observation, experimentation, and research. It plays a vital role in improving human life and technology Science seeks to understand the world through observation and experimentation, while technology applies that scientific knowledge to create tools and solutions for practical problems, forming a symbiotic relationship where science inspires new inventions, and technology provides tools for deeper scientific discovery, driving innovation in fields like medicine, communication, and computing. This interconnected cycle continually advances human capability, improving daily life while also posing new societal and ethical questions. .
